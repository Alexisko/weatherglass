    How Weatherglass computes its numbers

    How this is computed

    Period

    Tap a month, then a second month to choose a range.

    Temperature

    Average daytime high and night low, °C. Shaded: the range of most days (10th–90th percentile).

    • Average high
    • Average low
    • Most days

    Days with rain

    Average number of days a month with 1 mm of rain or more.

    Sunshine

    Average hours of sunshine a day, out of the hours of daylight.

    • Sunshine
    • Daylight

    Month by month

    For the selected period, precipitation and day counts are totals; temperatures and hours are averages.
